## 📖 Earnings Play Strategy Guide

### Play Types
- **📈 Pre-Earnings Run-Up**: Buy 5-10 days before earnings, sell the day before. Works best with bullish trend + historical positive bias.
- **🎯 Earnings Hold**: High-conviction directional bet through earnings. Requires strong trend alignment + consistent historical reaction.
- **⚡ Post-Earnings Drift**: Wait for the earnings reaction, then trade the continuation 1-5 days after. Lower risk, captures follow-through.
- **👀 Pre-Earnings Watch**: Setup is forming but not yet actionable. Monitor for entry triggers.
- **🚫 Earnings Avoid**: No clear edge — stay flat through this report.

### Risk Management
- **Pre-earnings positions**: Exit or reduce before the announcement unless running an explicit hold-through play
- **Position sizing**: HIGH conviction = standard size (2-5%), MED = half, LOW = quarter or skip
- **Stop discipline**: Pre-earnings stops are wider (1.5-2x ATR) since you're trading a known catalyst
- **Correlation risk**: If multiple stocks in the same sector report the same week, the first one's reaction often previews the rest
